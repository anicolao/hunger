Scale reference

One direction, every time

Choose the closest description. A number describes a body sensation; it is never a grade or a target.

1 Urgent hungerWeak, shaky, or dizzy may be present.
2 Strong hungerEmpty, irritable, or eager to eat.
3 Clear hungerReady to eat without urgency.
4 Early hungerSubtle body cues or more thoughts of food.
5 NeutralNeither hungry nor full.
6 Satisfied and comfortableHunger has eased.
7 Comfortably fullLittle interest in more food.
8 Too fullPressure or mild discomfort.
9 Very fullMarked discomfort.
10 Painfully fullNauseated or unwell may be present.
